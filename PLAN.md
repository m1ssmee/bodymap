# Your Body, Explained — hackathon plan (4 hours, Vercel, Gemini Flash)

One line: upload any medical record (prescription, blood report, X-ray, MRI, ultrasound) and every finding is pinned to a 3D body you can tap, explained in plain language.

## Status — 28 Oct 2026
Built and verified locally (build, lint, browser test on desktop and phone width):
- 3D hologram body with severity markers, tap a dot or a record to filter, region view across all records
- Upload (camera / file / PDF / two sample buttons) -> Gemini -> confirm screen -> saved to localStorage
- Record detail with "since last report" changes, doctor questions, read aloud
- Medicines (morning / afternoon / night, newest prescription wins), Trends (SVG charts with healthy band)
- Gemini route with key rotation, 413/400/422/503 errors handled
- Demo packet generated: public/demo + demo-docs/print

Live Gemini tested on all demo docs (blood report ~15 s, handwritten Rx ~35 s). Deployed: https://bodymap-pied.vercel.app
Not done: Hindi toggle. Keys 2 and 6 in healthyapi.txt need billing on their Google projects and were left out.

| Hindi/Kannada UI strings (~40 strings) | Hardcoded JSON, AI writes, you sanity-check | Both |

## Feature list

### Must ship
1. Upload: camera or file, JPG/PNG/PDF, multi-page PDF ok
2. Gemini extraction into one strict schema: record type, date, doctor, findings[] (title, plain explanation, severity, body region, source quote), medicines[] (name, dose, timing, before/after food, purpose), lab values[] (name, value, unit, range, flag), advice[], questions for doctor[]
3. Confirm screen before save: shows what was extracted, Confirm / Fix
4. 3D body: translucent human mesh, auto-rotate, drag to turn, glowing markers per finding coloured by severity, tap marker -> finding panel
5. Timeline strip under the body: records by date, tap to filter markers
6. Record detail: plain summary, "what changed since your last <same type>", questions for next visit
7. Medicines: Morning / Afternoon / Night blocks, purpose line, read-aloud per block
8. Trends: line per lab value across reports with reference band, one-sentence explanation
9. Demo patient preloaded on first visit (5 records, markers already on the body)
10. Local storage only, no login, "nothing leaves your phone except the image being read"
11. Gemini key rotation: comma-separated keys, round robin, retry once on 429
12. Large text, light theme, one action per screen, single disclaimer line in footer
13. Deployed on Vercel, mobile-first

### Nice to have (only if ahead of schedule)
14. Hindi or Kannada toggle for UI and explanations
15. Read-aloud on findings and record summaries (browser speechSynthesis)
16. Scan image viewer with Gemini-described regions of interest
17. Print / share summary as PDF

### Cut
Accounts, ABHA, calculators (BMI, BMR, etc.), drug interactions, emergency card, maternal/kidney/cycle modules, dark theme, organ-accurate anatomy meshes, raw-image diagnosis (app explains the report; image-only uploads are labelled preliminary)

Phone: photograph a blood report, confirm, liver marker lights up (SGPT high).
Medicines: tap Morning, it reads aloud.
Close: "Every report you've ever been given, in one body, explained. Nothing leaves your phone except the image being read."
